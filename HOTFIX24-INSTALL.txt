HOTFIX 24 — BOOKING RATE-ROW JOIN AND TERMINAL BREAKDOWN
=======================================================

around its embedded breakdown. Hotfix 24 joins each structured rate to its
bounded rendered table row by exact subtotal, then uses that row's stable block
id and cancellation policy. Successful quotes now print every fee, tax and
discount directly in the terminal as well as in the JSON report.
