What this adds
--------------
Hotfix 12 adds the strict Booking.com pricing parser, exact offline replay and
the Booking quote CLI route. It does NOT authorise a live Booking price test.

The tool now rejects unknown charges, ambiguous deposits, missing currencies,
missing itemisation and totals that do not reconcile. An ordinary live Booking
quote is locked before any browser or network request.
