HOTFIX 22 — BOOKING EMBEDDED FEE BREAKDOWN
==========================================

This patch reads Booking's exact structured rate breakdown from the dated
public listing page. It no longer needs to click Reserve for the supported
page shape.

It:

- selects one unambiguous free-cancellation rate;
- keeps only bounded accommodation, fee and tax fields;
- includes mandatory cleaning in the comparable price;
- excludes VAT and city/tourist tax;
- rejects unknown, conditional, excluded or unreconciled charges;
- replays the saved structured snapshot without network access.
