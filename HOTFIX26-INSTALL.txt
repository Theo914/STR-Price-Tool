HOTFIX 26 — BOOKING UNAVAILABLE-DATES DETECTOR
================================================

Hotfix 26 recognises Booking's public "next available dates" price cards as
evidence that the exact requested stay is unavailable when the page contains
no room rows and no structured rate. Alternative-date prices remain diagnostic
only and are never reported as the requested stay's price.

---------------------

  -DestinationPath "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\travelstaytion-parity-tool" `
  -Force

cd "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\travelstaytion-parity-tool"
