TravelStaytion parity tool — Hotfix 30
======================================

Purpose
-------
Fix TravelStaytion discount handling before parity comparison.

The live Villa Andrews response reported:

- pre-discount comparable total: EUR 1,552.18;
- long-stay discount: EUR 71.61;
- corrected comparable total: EUR 1,480.57;
- displayed total: EUR 1,480.58;
- rounding difference: EUR -0.01.

Hotfix 29 incorrectly compared Booking against the pre-discount amount, which
overstated Booking's saving as EUR 164.68 / 10.61%. With the discount applied,
the expected result for the captured evidence is EUR 93.07 / 6.29%.

The adapter now:

- prefers exact decimal discount, cleaning and deposit values;
- subtracts the TravelStaytion discount from its comparable total;
- distinguishes tax from discount when reconciling the displayed total;
- fails closed when the discounted arithmetic disagrees by more than EUR 0.02;
- records TravelStaytion accommodation, discount, cleaning and tax lines.
