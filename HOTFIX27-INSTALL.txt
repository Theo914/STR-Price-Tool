HOTFIX 27 — SAFE-DATE POLICY FOUNDATION
========================================

Hotfix 27 adds the first Phase 7 guardrails. Live `quote` commands now read the
current operating date and reject check-ins that have passed or are less than
30 days away. Exact offline fixture replay is unchanged. The core also produces
deterministic, bounded seven-night candidate windows around 60 days ahead for
the next availability-selection increment.

This is the Phase 7 foundation. It does not yet probe TravelStaytion to choose
the first available candidate window automatically.
