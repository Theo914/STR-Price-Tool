TravelStaytion parity tool - Phase 11 / Hotfix 39
=================================================

What this adds
--------------

The quote report now includes discreet, non-bookable what-if discount calculations:

- Airbnb: 10%
- Booking.com: 10%, 15% and 20%

Safety rules
------------

- A calculator is available only when the captured OTA price breakdown is exact and reconciled.
- The hypothetical percentage is applied to the captured accommodation component.
- Fixed cleaning fees and other fixed fees are not discounted.
- A tax is reduced only when its captured label explicitly states a percentage rate.
- An equivalent discount already present in the captured price is not stacked again.
- Every result is labelled hypothetical and not bookable.
- The adjusted OTA total is compared with the aligned TravelStaytion guest total in the same currency.

Install
-------

Open PowerShell and run:

Expand-Archive `
  -Path "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\parity-tool-phase11-discounts-hotfix39.zip" `
  -DestinationPath "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\travelstaytion-parity-tool" `
  -Force

cd "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\travelstaytion-parity-tool"

.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -q

Expected result: 234 passed.

Acceptance proof 1 - Airbnb
---------------------------

.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m parity.cli quote `
  "https://www.travelstaytion.com/stay/1013419" `
  --channel airbnb `
  --candidate-url "https://www.airbnb.com.au/rooms/1506734032830000497" `
  --db ".\.parity\uk-no-licence.db" `
  --checkin 2027-02-01 `
  --checkout 2027-02-04 `
  --guests 2 `
  --currency EUR `
  --offline `
  --fixtures-dir ".parity/airbnb-hoxton-hotfix37-minstay-fixtures" `
  --governor-db ".parity/airbnb-hoxton-hotfix39-offline-governor.db" `
  --format json `
  --output "quote-1013419-airbnb-hotfix39-offline.json"

Expected what-if line:

[parity]     10%: EUR 666.06 guest total; additional discount EUR 76.20
